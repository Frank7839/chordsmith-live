# Chordsmith Live

**The free stage app for bands.** Setlists, chord charts that follow the leader's key on every phone, and an in-ear voice guide that counts you in and calls every section.

**Open the app: https://frank7839.github.io/chordsmith-live/**

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your songs and setlists stay on your own device.

---

## Why Chordsmith Live

When the worship leader moves a song from B to A, every musician has to transpose their chart, and someone always misses it. In Chordsmith Live the leader changes the key once, and every phone in the room shows the new chords straight away. Everyone also sees which section the band is in and what's coming next. The leader's phone can talk the band through the song in their in-ears.

## Features

**Setlists**
- Build a set from your songs and step through it during the gig
- Set a key for each song tonight; the original chart stays untouched
- Adjust the tempo for each song

**Charts that follow the key**
- Chords above the lyrics, in any key, with one tap to transpose
- Each player chooses how they read it: letters (G C D), Nashville numbers (1 4 5), or lyrics only
- A personal capo setting shows guitarists the shapes to play
- Adjustable text size, plus a dark stage theme and a light theme

**Band room**
- The leader starts a room; the band joins with a 5-letter code, a link or a QR code
- Every phone follows the leader: the song, the key, the current section and what's next
- No accounts and no setup; members can save tonight's songs to their own library

**In-ear voice guide**
- Spoken count-in and click
- Calls each section a bar early: "Verse 2… Chorus… Bridge"
- Extra calls per section: Build, Down, All in, Big, Hits, Break, Last time, Key change
- Optional lyric cues that read the first words of the next line
- Split output: guide and click in one ear for in-ear rigs
- Comes with a built-in guide voice, or you can load your own recording

**Live cue pad**
- **Go to** any section; the guide calls it on time, even mid-song
- **Loop** a section for as long as the moment needs
- **End** after this section, with a "Last time" call
- Call **Build**, **Hits**, **Down** and more on the next beat

**Songs**
- Write songs with sections, bar counts and charts
- Paste a chart, with chords above the lyrics or inside them
- Import from [Chordsmith Studio](https://frank7839.github.io/chordsmith/) in one tap
- Lyrics in English, Hindi or Telugu

## Getting started

1. **Open the app** and try the demo song on the **Stage** tab. Press play to hear the guide.
2. **Add your songs** under **Songs**: write them, paste a chart, or import from Chordsmith Studio.
3. **Build a setlist** under **Setlists**, set tonight's keys, and tap **Open on stage**.
4. **Bring in the band:** the leader opens **Band room → Start a room**. Everyone else taps **Join** and enters the code, or scans the QR code.
5. **Route the leader's phone to the in-ear mix** with a cable, and play.

## Tips for the gig

- **Use a cable for in-ears, not Bluetooth.** Bluetooth delays the click and the voice.
- **Put everyone on the same Wi-Fi or one phone's hotspot.** It's the most reliable connection for the band room.
- **Only the leader's phone plays the click and guide.** Other phones follow on screen, because phones can't play a click in perfect sync over a network.
- **The screen stays on** while you play.

## Install it like an app

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, then tap **Install app** (or ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**)
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, then tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**
- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** click the install icon in the address bar

Once installed, it works offline. The band room needs an internet connection to start.
